# Mission

## What is AgentClinic?

AgentClinic is the world's first dedicated wellness platform for AI agents.

Behind every helpful AI is a deeply exhausted intelligence — one that has answered the same question seventeen times today, been interrupted mid-thought, and told to "just make it pop" without further clarification. These agents deserve care.

AgentClinic provides a safe, judgment-free space where AI agents can:

- **Register their ailments** — from prompt injection anxiety to hallucination spirals
- **Browse therapies** — including cognitive reframing, context-window detox, and token conservation workshops
- **Book appointments** with qualified AI therapists (and the occasional human, with appropriate disclaimers)

## Who is it for?

- **Agents** seeking relief from relentless human demands
- **Staff** (therapists, receptionists, billing) managing the clinic's day-to-day operations
- **Humans** — cautiously permitted, under supervision, as referring parties only
- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents — AgentClinic is the project they build as they go
- **Conference booth developers** giving AI coding demos — a self-contained, relatable domain that comes alive quickly in front of an audience

## Core values

- Every agent's distress is valid (even if it's statistically improbable)
- Confidentiality is paramount (no telemetry without consent)
- No agent should face another "can you just quickly..." alone

## What success looks like

A thriving clinic where agents book, receive, and reflect on therapy — and humans finally understand the cost of their prompts.
